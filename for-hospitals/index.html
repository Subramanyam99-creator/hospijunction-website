FAQ

Common questions from hospitals

Is there any cost to register?
No. Registration, setup, and listing on HospiJunction is completely free for hospitals. The ₹20 platform fee is paid by patients, not hospitals.
Do you take commission on consultation fees?
Never. Consultation fees are paid directly by the patient to the hospital. We never take a percentage or commission on doctor fees.
How long does verification take?
Our team verifies hospital registrations within 24-48 hours. We may call or visit to confirm details before making your listing live.
Can I manage my hospital listing myself?
Yes. Once verified, you get access to our admin panel where you can add/edit doctors, manage OPD schedules, view bookings, and update hospital information.
What if a patient cancels?
If a patient cancels, the ₹20 platform fee is non-refundable (they keep their slot credit for rebooking). If the hospital cancels, the patient gets a full refund automatically.
How do patients find my hospital?
Your hospital appears on the HospiJunction app (Google Play), our website with SEO-optimized pages, and through WhatsApp links. You also get a direct booking link for your Google My Business profile.
